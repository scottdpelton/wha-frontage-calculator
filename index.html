    About the costs, sources and limits
    Working draft. City rules and sources checked November 20, 2026. Bellingham has not supplied unit costs, so cost to build uses the average of three 2025 Whatcom County bids (Ferndale, Lynden) for bid items, plus contractor overhead (mobilization, traffic control, surveying) at the share four 2025 Whatcom County bids spent on it, design engineering (state DOT proxy), a performance bond, Bellingham's 1.5% administration deposit, and a 5% change-order contingency. Street trees, clearing and planter-strip restoration come from the same bids; record drawings from a 2024 Federal Way bid. Not priced: the street light and, where over about 2,000 sq ft of new hard surface, soils testing. The street light is not priced. Paving and stormwater also rest on an assumed street section, width and drainage design, labeled under Unit costs. Every rate is editable under Unit costs.
